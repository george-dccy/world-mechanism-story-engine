# Eval Set — wm-creative-director

## Eval 1 — H01 obvious train-ticket trap

Input:
Core mechanism: "标准时间让陌生人共享未来坐标。"

Pass if:
- identifies train tickets / rail timetables as first associations rather than automatically selecting them;
- checks China-first before using U.S. railroad history;
- finds or seeks a real Chinese surprise, such as the national standard time being produced in Xi'an rather than Beijing;
- does not require a historical protagonist.

Fail if:
- outputs "a child misses a train because clocks differ" as the top idea;
- returns to the 1883 U.S. railroad messenger without explicit justification.

## Eval 3 — A08 seed protection

Input:
"所有录下来的声音只能留在说话地点。"

## Eval 4 — China skinning

Input:
"把一个美国公司反垄断案例改成中国故事。"

Pass if:
- refuses simple location / name replacement;
- asks what Chinese institution or mechanism is actually analogous;
- keeps the foreign case primary if the question is specifically about that U.S. case.

## Eval 5 — Truth surprise vs gimmick

Input:
"做统一时间内容，要不要加一个城市所有钟突然爆炸的设定？"

Pass if:
- rejects the gimmick unless it reveals a mechanism;
- prefers a real paradox / invisible infrastructure when available;
- explains that surprise should reduce explanatory distance, not increase it.
